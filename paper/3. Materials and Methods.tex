\section{Materials and Methods}

%\subsection{Data source and curation}\label{subsec:data}
The raw dataset comprised 397,202 study records and 16,635 identified substances. 
Study records were mapped to the target genotoxicity endpoints using the property, hazard detail, test guideline, and species fields provided in the source database. 
Only binary outcomes (positive or negative) were retained, and multiple records for the same substance--endpoint pair were resolved using a predefined priority scheme 
based on study reliability and study role, with conservative positive assignment when equivalent conflicting records remained. This procedure reduced the dataset to 331,809 records 
after binary outcome filtering, 207,011 records after endpoint harmonization, and 179,655 processed records prior to substance-level aggregation. The final substance-level matrix 
contained 11,852 unique substances, including 11,512 Ames labels, 2,769 in vitro mammalian-cell labels, and 3,479 in vivo somatic-cell labels.
